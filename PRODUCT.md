# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, admissions reviewers, robotics mentors, and employers evaluating Mehrdad Shariatmadari, a grade 11 student in Toronto aiming for mechatronics engineering. They arrive from a resume link or LinkedIn, skim in under two minutes, and decide whether the work is real.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that proves hands-on engineering ability with real artifacts: CAD renders, dimensioned drawings, demo videos, competition robotics roles, and work history. Success is a visitor remembering one specific project (the Kalimbinator, the Chopstick Ring) rather than a vibe.

## Brand Personality

Precise, hands-on, understated. The voice of someone who builds physical things and lets them speak. Confident without selling.

## Anti-references

- AI-generated SaaS landing aesthetics: ambient color glows, gradient pills, eyebrow labels above every section, colored card borders, gradient text. All were deliberately stripped; never reintroduce.
- Template portfolio grammar: icon-in-circle decorations, identical card grids, hero metrics.
- Decorative novelty fonts for the name (Bitcount was tried and rejected).

## Design Principles

1. **Engineered, not decorated.** Visual interest comes from typography, spacing, and real project artifacts, never from ambient effects.
2. **Show the work.** Projects lead with actual assets: CAD sheets, renders, working-demo video. No abstractions where a real image exists.
3. **Quiet surfaces, loud content.** Near-black canvas, neutral borders, restrained accent use; the name, the roles, and the builds carry the page.
4. **Motion earns its place.** One orchestrated intro (name build-out), restrained scroll reveals, swipeable experience deck. Reduced motion is always respected.
5. **Specific beats general.** Copy names real teams (FRC 7902), real pieces (Come Sunday), real dimensions (208 mm tip-to-ring).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on the dark theme. Keyboard-reachable navigation and carousels. `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for the intro screen and all reveals. Alt text describes the actual artifact.
